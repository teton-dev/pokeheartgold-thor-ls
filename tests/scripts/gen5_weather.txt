# A battle starts in the weather the field is in (HGSS's does; the Battle Arcade's roulette sets one too): rain (1) and a
# sandstorm (7), in BW2's engine. The check is the shots: the rain, the sandstorm and the touch screen's weather badge.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_weather.txt --from new-game
mash A until free
give 155 5
debug weather 1
wild 19 5 gen5
until battle timeout 600
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
shot rain-command
mash touch 128 183 until free timeout 6000
debug weather 7
wild 19 5 gen5
until battle timeout 600
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
shot sand-command
exit
