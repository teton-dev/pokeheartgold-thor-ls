# The Gear's Area and Map tabs with Nuzlocke mode in Goldenrod: Route 30 on Hard with a scripted wild Pidgey (which counts
# only with Hard) that the script's touches catch, so the tabs show blue "Catch complete" with the Pidgey in colour (Gen 5's
# sprite, with the ROM imported). The script ends by waiting, so that the lower window can be looked at and tapped by hand
# (desktop.sh shot / click on the run's container; Full, then Map). The "[test] gear catch status" lines: available, caught,
# caught. (The yellow and red states are checked in Eterna's nuzlocke_gear_hard.txt; the status logic is shared.)
#
#   SCREEN=1800x1300 SIM_NATIVE_SETTINGS=1 SIM_NATIVE_SETTINGS_EVENTS='toggle @Show the Gear 1|close' \
#     TEST_CONFIG='SecondaryScreen=true BottomWindowWidth=540 BottomWindowHeight=620' tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_gear_view.txt --from new-game

gen5-import
mash A until free
settings Gear
wait 120
nuzlocke hard
give 155 5
editor item 4 10
wait 40
warp 34 553 340
until free timeout 3000
wait 90
nuzlocke state
wild 16 3 gen5
until battle timeout 600
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
mash touch 128 183 until free timeout 6000
nuzlocke state
nuzlocke normal
wait 40
nuzlocke state
wait 400000
exit 0
