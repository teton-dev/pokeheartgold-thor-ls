# Nuzlocke mode's alpha mark (docs/thor/dev/nuzlocke.md, "Milestone 5"): out in New Bark Town, the X menu's SAVE
# prompt has an info window with a line of the mark, "NUZLOCKE" and the level (the check is the stillshots); the
# game is saved (by the test command) and kept as a Nuzlocke fixture, whose main menu nuzlocke_marks_menu.txt checks.
#
#   tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/nuzlocke_marks.txt --from new-game --keep nuz-normal
mash A until free
nuzlocke normal
walk LEFT 3
walk UP 2
walk LEFT 1
mash A until map 63 timeout 3000
walk DOWN 7
walk DOWN 1
mash A until map 60 timeout 3000
wait 60
press X
wait 60
press RIGHT
wait 20
press UP
wait 20
stillshot menu_save
press A
wait 120
stillshot save_prompt
mash B until free
save
nuzlocke state
exit 0
