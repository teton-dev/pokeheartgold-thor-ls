# From new-game: the Safari Zone in BW2's engine, by HGSS's rules (the same as Platinum's: gen5/src/safari.c). A level
# 5 Cyndaquil in the party (none of it on the field), a level 22 Bulbasaur (HGSS's flee rate 0: it never flees) with
# 30 Safari Balls: the command screen (BALL, BAIT, ROCK, RUN), a ball, bait, mud, then RUN; passes once back on the
# overworld with 29 balls.
#
#   TEST_ENV=POKEPLATINUM_GEN5_SAFARI_SEED=3 tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_safari.txt --from new-game
mash A until free
give 155 5
gen5 on
safari 1 22 30
until battle timeout 600
wait 150
stillshot intro
until command timeout 6000
wait 60
stillshot command
# BALL (the middle button): the throw, the result, the Pokemon's turn
touch 128 80
wait 100
stillshot ball1
wait 90
stillshot ball2
mash B until command timeout 6000
wait 60
# BAIT (the left button)
touch 40 178
wait 120
stillshot bait
mash B until command timeout 6000
wait 60
# MUD (the right button)
touch 216 178
wait 120
stillshot mud
mash B until command timeout 6000
wait 60
# RUN, at the bottom of the touch screen, with B through the messages
touch 128 178
mash B touch 128 178 until free timeout 6000
stillshot back
editor safari 29
exit
