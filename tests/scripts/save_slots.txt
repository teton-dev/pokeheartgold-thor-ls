# port-common's save slots, autosave and the Saves page (Settings > Saves, as the Platinum fork's). From new-game: on
# the main menu, R loads slot 2 (empty: the menu without CONTINUE, its NEW GAME) and L slot 1 again (CONTINUE with
# "SLOT 1/4"); then into the game, the Saves page through the native settings' recorder, and its "Save now".
# The check is the stillshots and the log: the page's cards ("Slot 1: ..."), "[native-settings] event tap @Save now"
# and the saved notice.
#
#   SCREEN=1920x1080 SIM_NATIVE_SETTINGS=1 SIM_NATIVE_SETTINGS_EVENTS='tap @Save now|close' \
#       tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/save_slots.txt --from new-game

wait 300
press A
wait 200
press A
wait 300
stillshot menu-slot1
press R
wait 400
stillshot menu-slot2
press L
wait 400
stillshot menu-slot1-again
mash A until free
settings Saves
wait 60
exit 0
