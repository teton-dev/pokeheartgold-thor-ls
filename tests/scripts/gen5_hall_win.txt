# A won Battle Hall battle in BW2's engine (HGSS): gen5_hall.txt's setup with a Spiritomb given Wonder Guard and Dark
# Pulse by the save editor. The check is the room menu's shot: no RECORD entry (the port leaves it out after a Gen 5
# battle).
#
#   SCREEN=1920x1080 tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_hall_win.txt --from new-game

mash A until free
gen5 on
give 442 60
# Spiritomb (party slot 0: the new game starts with no Pokemon, and the party screen's cursor starts on it) with Wonder Guard (Ghost and Dark: no weaknesses,
# so no damaging move touches it) and Dark Pulse in every slot: it wins by mashing A whatever the opponent's team
editor ability 0 25
editor allmoves 0 399
give 6 60
warp 276 7 9
until free timeout 3000
wait 30
press LEFT
wait 20
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 90
press A
wait 150
press A
wait 150
stillshot sel
mash A until command timeout 30000
wait 60
shot battle-command
# Dark Pulse until the trainer's Pokémon is down, then on to the room's menu
mash A until menu timeout 40000
wait 60
shot room-menu
exit 0
