# From new-game: the Safari Zone's endings in BW2's engine. A level 22 Kangaskhan (HGSS's flee rate 150: bait makes
# it run off soon) baited until it flees; then a level 22 Bulbasaur with the last ball (catch rate 45: the seed makes
# it break out), "out of balls", and HGSS's Safari encounter takes the player back to the gate (here: where the
# battle started).
#
#   TEST_ENV=POKEPLATINUM_GEN5_SAFARI_SEED=3 tools/docker/desktop.sh test tests/scripts/gen5_safari_end.txt --from new-game
mash A until free
give 155 5
gen5 on
safari 115 22 30
until battle timeout 600
until command timeout 6000
wait 90
# BAIT, over and over, until it runs away
touch 40 178
mash B touch 40 178 until free timeout 6000
stillshot fled
editor safari 30
safari 1 22 1
until battle timeout 600
until command timeout 6000
wait 90
# BALL, the only one
touch 128 80
wait 400
stillshot out
mash B until free timeout 8000
stillshot back
editor safari 0
exit
